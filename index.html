Who we are

A calling born from compassion, rooted in faith.

God First Charity Foundation began as a personal conviction — to extend a helping hand to the many elderly, orphans and homeless children across Mayuge and the wider Busoga region who lack the basic necessities of life: food, shelter, clothing and medical care.

Many of the elderly cannot afford these needs on their own. Some have children who have abandoned them. Children roam the streets without guidance. We believe every person, regardless of age or status, deserves a life of dignity.

"Whoever is kind to the poor lends to the Lord, and He will reward them for what they have done." — Proverbs 19:17

Today we are a registered Community Based Organization supervised by a ministry director and board, championing the rights and interests of the most vulnerable — putting them first.

Our values — H · T · P · F

The heart behind everything we do.

Four values guide our conduct, our relationships with beneficiaries, partners and each other. They define what is right, and who we are.

H

Hard Work

Tirelessly serving others with commitment, love and determination — sacrificing time, energy and resources to improve lives.

T

Teamwork

Working together, sharing ideas, responsibilities, skills and support so that the charity can help more people, more effectively.

P

Passion

A strong love and deep concern for helping others — especially those in greatest need, when no one else is looking.

F

Family

A group of people who care for, support and protect one another like relatives — showing love, unity, compassion and responsibility.
